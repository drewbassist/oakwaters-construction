Mudrooms Made for Ontario Weather

Design an entry that handles wet coats, footwear, and everyday transitions.

Retrospective topic: July 2024 · Oakwaters Journal

Winter salt, damp boots, and bulky outerwear place unusual demands on a busy entrance.

Design for wet boots and winter layers

Plan a durable floor, washable surfaces, drip space for footwear and accessible hooks for coats. Allow room for more than one person to arrive at once.

Keep storage easy to maintain

Combine open everyday storage with closed cabinets for seasonal gear. Consider ventilation, lighting and a place to sit when removing boots.

Planning prompt: Write down three non-negotiable outcomes for the project, then ask which construction decisions are necessary to achieve each one.
