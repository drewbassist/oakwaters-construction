A Kitchen Layout That Works Harder

Evaluate circulation, preparation zones, storage, and lighting before choosing cabinet finishes.

Retrospective topic: November 2023 · Oakwaters Journal

A beautiful kitchen can still frustrate its owners when paths cross and frequently used items are hard to reach.

Map the daily kitchen workflow

Test the route between refrigerator, sink, preparation space, cooktop and dishwasher. Consider where groceries arrive and where more than one person will work without blocking a doorway.

Plan appliance clearances early

Confirm appliance specifications, door swings, ventilation and electrical locations before cabinetry is finalized. A beautiful plan is not useful if the dishwasher blocks the main walkway.

Planning prompt: Write down three non-negotiable outcomes for the project, then ask which construction decisions are necessary to achieve each one.
