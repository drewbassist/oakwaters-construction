Planning a Renovation Before Winter

Create a clear scope, establish priorities, and identify decisions that must happen before demolition.

Retrospective topic: October 2023 · Oakwaters Journal

A renovation is easier to manage when the team knows which rooms must remain usable and which selections affect the schedule.

Identify weather-sensitive construction tasks

Exterior openings, roofing, masonry and some finishes depend on temperature and moisture conditions. Discuss sequencing and temporary weather protection before scheduling work.

Prepare for household disruption

Confirm material lead times, heating continuity, access and safe temporary routes. Build flexibility into the schedule for weather-related delays.

Planning prompt: Write down three non-negotiable outcomes for the project, then ask which construction decisions are necessary to achieve each one.
