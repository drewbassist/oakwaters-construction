The Case for Better Storage

Treat storage as part of the architecture rather than an afterthought.

Retrospective topic: April 2024 · Oakwaters Journal

Clutter often reflects a mismatch between the home and the activities taking place inside it.

Inventory what needs a home

Group items by frequency of use and size before designing cabinets. Storage is most effective when the contents are easy to see, reach and return.

Use overlooked spaces carefully

Consider drawers, tall cabinets and under-stair storage while preserving ventilation, access panels and required clearances. Avoid filling every wall at the expense of daylight.

Planning prompt: Write down three non-negotiable outcomes for the project, then ask which construction decisions are necessary to achieve each one.
