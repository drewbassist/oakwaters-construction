What Makes a Great Laundry Room

Improve workflow with counter space, storage, and durable finishes.

Retrospective topic: November 2025 · Oakwaters Journal

Laundry rooms are small workspaces with demanding practical requirements.

Plan around the laundry workflow

Provide room to sort, treat stains, wash, dry, fold and store supplies. Confirm appliance door swings, ventilation and convenient access to power and plumbing.

Prepare for leaks and vibration

Discuss appropriate flooring, water shutoffs, drain provisions where feasible and appliance clearances. Upper cabinets should remain reachable without interfering with machines.

Planning prompt: Write down three non-negotiable outcomes for the project, then ask which construction decisions are necessary to achieve each one.
