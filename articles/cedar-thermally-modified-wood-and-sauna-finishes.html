Cedar, Thermally Modified Wood and Sauna Finishes

Choosing materials for heat and humidity.

Oakwaters Construction · Southern Ontario guide

Wood species and surface treatments influence the atmosphere of a sauna, but appearance alone should not drive selection. Interior bench surfaces must be suitable for direct skin contact in high temperatures and should remain comfortable and serviceable.

Plan the construction details

Compare appropriate sauna-grade woods for dimensional stability, splinter resistance, availability and maintenance. Evaluate exterior cladding separately: Ontario freeze-thaw cycles, sun exposure and rain demand robust flashing and drainage details.

Think about long-term use

Keep fasteners and exposed hardware suitable for the environment. Request product-specific installation guidance and avoid applying ordinary interior finishes to surfaces that will be heated beyond their intended service conditions.

Before starting work

Discuss the intended use, site conditions, materials, construction sequence and maintenance expectations with the project team. Verify permits and applicable requirements with the local municipality and qualified trades before ordering materials or beginning work. The best result comes from resolving practical details while the design is still flexible.
