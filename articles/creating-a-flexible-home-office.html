Creating a Flexible Home Office

Design for concentration, video calls, storage, and future uses.

Retrospective topic: January 2025 · Oakwaters Journal

A spare bedroom converted to an office should still support comfortable work over long periods.

Design around the work you actually do

List the equipment, calls, meetings and storage you need. Position the desk for useful daylight without glare on screens, and plan accessible power and data connections.

Build in acoustic flexibility

Doors, soft finishes and strategic layout can reduce interruptions. Consider whether the space may later become a guest room or study.

Planning prompt: Write down three non-negotiable outcomes for the project, then ask which construction decisions are necessary to achieve each one.
