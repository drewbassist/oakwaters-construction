Meet Brennan Waters: The Person Behind Oakwaters Construction

Built on trust. Backed by experience. A personal introduction to the owner of Oakwaters Construction.

Oakwaters Journal · October 2026

Every construction project begins with trust. At Oakwaters Construction, owner Brennan Waters believes homeowners deserve clear communication, careful planning, and respect for the properties they entrust to his team.

A personal commitment

“I believe great work begins with trust, honest communication, and respect for the people who invite us into their homes. My experience has taught me that the details matter—from the first conversation to the final walkthrough. I take pride in doing things properly, keeping my word, and treating your property as if it were my own.”

— Brennan Waters, Owner, Oakwaters Construction

The details matter

Renovations and excavation work involve significant decisions about scope, budgets, schedules, and site conditions. Oakwaters Construction emphasizes straightforward conversations and responsible planning so clients understand the work being considered.

Renovation and excavation in Ontario

From improving an existing home to preparing a property for new construction, Oakwaters Construction provides renovation and excavation services for Ontario property owners.
