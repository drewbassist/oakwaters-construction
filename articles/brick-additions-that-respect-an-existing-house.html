Brick Additions That Respect an Existing House

Matching new masonry to old.

Oakwaters Construction · Southern Ontario guide

A brick addition should connect visually and technically with the original house. Matching colour alone is rarely enough; unit size, bond pattern, joint profile and weathering affect the result.

Plan the construction details

Survey existing conditions and movement joints before choosing a connection detail. New foundations may settle differently from older construction, and wall ties, flashing and drainage must suit the assembly.

Think about long-term use

Test sample bricks and mortar on site under natural light. Where a perfect match is impossible, a deliberate complementary design may age better than a near match that highlights every difference.

Before starting work

Discuss the intended use, site conditions, materials, construction sequence and maintenance expectations with the project team. Verify permits and applicable requirements with the local municipality and qualified trades before ordering materials or beginning work. The best result comes from resolving practical details while the design is still flexible.
