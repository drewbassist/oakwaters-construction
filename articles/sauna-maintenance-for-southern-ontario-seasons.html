Sauna Maintenance for Southern Ontario Seasons

Keeping the retreat durable year after year.

Oakwaters Construction · Southern Ontario guide

A sauna should be designed so routine inspection is easy. Moisture, leaves, snow and seasonal temperature changes affect the exterior even when the interior is operating as intended.

Plan the construction details

Inspect roof drainage, flashing, door seals, exterior cladding and the condition of steps after winter. Follow the heater manufacturer’s maintenance and ventilation instructions, and keep air openings unobstructed.

Think about long-term use

Allow interior wood surfaces to dry after use, clean benches with suitable products and watch for persistent dampness or odours. A simple seasonal checklist helps identify small problems before they become expensive repairs.

Before starting work

Discuss the intended use, site conditions, materials, construction sequence and maintenance expectations with the project team. Verify permits and applicable requirements with the local municipality and qualified trades before ordering materials or beginning work. The best result comes from resolving practical details while the design is still flexible.
