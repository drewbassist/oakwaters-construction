Windows, Daylight, and Renovation Design

Plan window changes around comfort, proportion, and performance.

Retrospective topic: December 2024 · Oakwaters Journal

A larger window can brighten a room but may also alter privacy, furniture placement, and heat gain.

Study daylight before changing openings

Observe where direct sun and glare occur throughout the day. Window size and placement affect privacy, furniture layouts, overheating and the character of the exterior.

Coordinate performance and installation

Compare insulation values, solar gain, ventilation and condensation control. Altering openings can require structural work and careful flashing.

Planning prompt: Write down three non-negotiable outcomes for the project, then ask which construction decisions are necessary to achieve each one.
