Planning a Home Addition

Think about structure, services, circulation, and the relationship to the existing home.

Retrospective topic: February 2024 · Oakwaters Journal

An addition succeeds when the new space feels like part of the original house rather than a disconnected attachment.

Test whether an addition solves the right problem

Compare the benefits of added square footage against reworking existing rooms. Consider site access, setbacks, daylight and how the addition connects to daily circulation.

Plan approvals and integration

Zoning, permits, structural connections, foundation design and mechanical capacity can shape feasibility. A cohesive addition also needs careful roof, drainage and exterior detailing.

Planning prompt: Write down three non-negotiable outcomes for the project, then ask which construction decisions are necessary to achieve each one.
