Preparing Your Home for an Exterior Upgrade

Sequence repairs and finish work for a durable result.

Retrospective topic: August 2026 · Oakwaters Journal

Exterior renovations often uncover water-management issues that should be addressed before new finishes go on.

Inspect before choosing finishes

Review siding, trim, gutters, windows and visible signs of water intrusion. Correcting substrate or flashing problems is often more important than changing the surface colour.

Coordinate work at intersections

Window edges, roof-to-wall junctions, decks and penetrations require careful detailing. Plan how each trade will maintain a continuous weather barrier.

Planning prompt: Write down three non-negotiable outcomes for the project, then ask which construction decisions are necessary to achieve each one.
