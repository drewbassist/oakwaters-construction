Choosing the Right Flooring for Each Room

Match flooring to moisture exposure, traffic, comfort, and maintenance.

Retrospective topic: March 2025 · Oakwaters Journal

A material that performs beautifully in a bedroom may be less suitable for an entry or basement.

Match flooring to the room conditions

Assess moisture, subfloor flatness, traffic and temperature variation. Entryways, basements, kitchens and bedrooms can have very different performance needs.

Plan transitions and installation height

Confirm underlayment, stair details, door clearance and connections between rooms before materials are ordered. Flooring thickness can affect cabinets and thresholds.

Planning prompt: Write down three non-negotiable outcomes for the project, then ask which construction decisions are necessary to achieve each one.
