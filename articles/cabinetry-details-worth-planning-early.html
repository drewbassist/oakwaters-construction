Cabinetry Details Worth Planning Early

Focus on internal organization, clearances, and hardware quality.

Retrospective topic: July 2025 · Oakwaters Journal

Cabinet door style is only one part of how a kitchen performs.

Specify the inside of every cabinet

Inventory cookware, pantry goods, cleaning supplies and small appliances. Plan drawer widths, pull-outs, waste bins and outlets before the cabinet order is placed.

Coordinate cabinetry with construction

Confirm finished floor heights, appliance dimensions, fillers, door swings and wall conditions. Small measurement errors can affect countertop fabrication and installation.

Planning prompt: Write down three non-negotiable outcomes for the project, then ask which construction decisions are necessary to achieve each one.
