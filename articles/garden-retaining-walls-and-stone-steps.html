Garden Retaining Walls and Stone Steps

Managing elevation changes gracefully.

Oakwaters Construction · Southern Ontario guide

A retaining wall can turn an awkward slope into usable garden space. Unlike a decorative border, a retaining structure must resist soil pressure and manage water behind the wall.

Plan the construction details

Wall height, surcharge loads, soil conditions and proximity to buildings influence engineering and permit requirements. Drainage aggregate, outlets and foundation preparation are fundamental, not optional extras.

Think about long-term use

Design steps with consistent risers, secure edges and lighting. Planting can soften the stonework, but roots, irrigation and runoff should not compromise the wall or adjacent structures.

Before starting work

Discuss the intended use, site conditions, materials, construction sequence and maintenance expectations with the project team. Verify permits and applicable requirements with the local municipality and qualified trades before ordering materials or beginning work. The best result comes from resolving practical details while the design is still flexible.
