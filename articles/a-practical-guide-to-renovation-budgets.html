A Practical Guide to Renovation Budgets

Build a budget around scope, allowances, and uncertainty.

Retrospective topic: June 2024 · Oakwaters Journal

A single headline price does not explain which fixtures, finishes, or site conditions have been included.

Build a scope-based renovation budget

Ask for a written breakdown of demolition, structural work, plumbing, electrical, finishes, labour, permits and cleanup where applicable. Separate fixed selections from allowances and identify which items are excluded.

Protect the budget against surprises

Older homes may conceal water damage, outdated wiring or uneven framing. Discuss a contingency appropriate to the project and document how discoveries, approvals and change orders affect the price and schedule.

Planning prompt: Write down three non-negotiable outcomes for the project, then ask which construction decisions are necessary to achieve each one.
