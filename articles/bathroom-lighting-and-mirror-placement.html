Bathroom Lighting and Mirror Placement

Coordinate illumination, reflection, and electrical planning.

Retrospective topic: April 2026 · Oakwaters Journal

Poorly positioned lights can cast shadows exactly where you need clear visibility.

Layer bathroom lighting around the mirror

Use task lighting that illuminates faces evenly rather than relying only on a ceiling fixture. Consider mirror width, mounting height, glare and the location of outlets before rough-in.

Match fixtures to wet locations

Lighting near tubs and showers must be suitable for the installation location and applicable electrical requirements. Coordinate switches, ventilation and dimming with the electrician.
