Planning a Primary Suite Renovation

Coordinate sleeping, bathing, dressing, storage, and privacy.

Retrospective topic: July 2026 · Oakwaters Journal

A primary suite should feel restful while supporting the practical routines of daily life.

Start with the complete suite layout

Map the relationship between bedroom, closet and bathroom. Evaluate privacy, circulation and storage before selecting individual fixtures or finishes.

Coordinate comfort systems

Consider sound separation, ventilation, lighting controls and temperature balance. Plumbing relocation and wet-area waterproofing should be resolved early.

Planning prompt: Write down three non-negotiable outcomes for the project, then ask which construction decisions are necessary to achieve each one.
