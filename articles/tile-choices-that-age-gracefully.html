Tile Choices That Age Gracefully

Balance visual character with slip resistance, grout maintenance, and scale.

Retrospective topic: November 2024 · Oakwaters Journal

Large-format tile can simplify a room visually, while smaller tile may be useful on sloped shower floors.

Match tile to location and use

Compare slip resistance, water exposure, grout maintenance and the suitability of each product for floors, showers or walls. Large-format tile may demand a flatter substrate.

Plan the installation layout

Review grout joints, transitions, niches, edges and pattern alignment before installation. Waterproofing behind wet-area tile matters more than the tile appearance alone.

Planning prompt: Write down three non-negotiable outcomes for the project, then ask which construction decisions are necessary to achieve each one.
