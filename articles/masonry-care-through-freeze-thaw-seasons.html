Masonry Care Through Freeze-Thaw Seasons

Protecting stone and brick from winter damage.

Oakwaters Construction · Southern Ontario guide

Water is one of the biggest threats to exterior masonry in Ontario. When saturated materials freeze and thaw repeatedly, surface scaling, cracking and joint deterioration can accelerate.

Plan the construction details

Keep gutters, downspouts and site drainage functioning so water is not concentrated against walls and steps. Use de-icing products only where compatible with the installed materials and follow supplier guidance.

Think about long-term use

Inspect after winter for loose units, failed joints, ponding and efflorescence. Clean with methods suited to the particular masonry; aggressive pressure washing and incompatible sealers can cause more harm than they solve.

Before starting work

Discuss the intended use, site conditions, materials, construction sequence and maintenance expectations with the project team. Verify permits and applicable requirements with the local municipality and qualified trades before ordering materials or beginning work. The best result comes from resolving practical details while the design is still flexible.
