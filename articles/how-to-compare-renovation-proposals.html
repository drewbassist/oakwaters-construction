How to Compare Renovation Proposals

Look beyond the total to understand what is actually being offered.

Retrospective topic: August 2024 · Oakwaters Journal

Two estimates can differ substantially because they assume different materials, preparation work, and responsibilities.

Compare identical scopes of work

Check whether proposals include the same demolition, materials, permits, disposal, finishes and site protection. A lower number may simply exclude items another contractor has priced.

Review allowances and change procedures

Ask which selections are provisional, how changes are approved, what payment milestones mean and who coordinates subcontractors. Compare clarity and responsibilities, not price alone.

Planning prompt: Write down three non-negotiable outcomes for the project, then ask which construction decisions are necessary to achieve each one.
