How to Build a Renovation Wish List

Translate inspiration images into a clear hierarchy of needs.

Retrospective topic: March 2026 · Oakwaters Journal

A collection of attractive photographs does not automatically become a buildable scope.

Separate needs, preferences and future ideas

Write down daily frustrations first, then rank outcomes as essential, desirable or optional. Identify who uses each space and what a successful renovation must change.

Bring a decision-ready brief

Collect room measurements, photographs, inspiration images and a preliminary budget range. A clear brief helps a contractor distinguish scope from aesthetic preferences.

Planning prompt: Write down three non-negotiable outcomes for the project, then ask which construction decisions are necessary to achieve each one.
