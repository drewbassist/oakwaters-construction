Renovating While Living at Home

Plan around dust, noise, access, and temporary routines.

Retrospective topic: February 2025 · Oakwaters Journal

Remaining in the house during construction can be practical, but it requires clear expectations.

Plan safe separation from construction

Discuss dust containment, daily cleanup, site access, temporary barriers and which rooms will remain usable. Clarify how utilities will be interrupted.

Create a practical household schedule

Identify when the kitchen, bathrooms or laundry will be unavailable. Set communication expectations and decide whether temporary accommodation is necessary for disruptive phases.

Planning prompt: Write down three non-negotiable outcomes for the project, then ask which construction decisions are necessary to achieve each one.
