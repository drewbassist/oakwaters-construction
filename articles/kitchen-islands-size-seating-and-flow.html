Kitchen Islands: Size, Seating, and Flow

Choose island dimensions that preserve movement and useful work surfaces.

Retrospective topic: October 2024 · Oakwaters Journal

An oversized island can make a kitchen feel smaller and create awkward traffic patterns.

Measure circulation around the island

Mark the footprint on the floor and test routes when appliance doors and drawers are open. Seating overhangs and occupied stools require additional space beyond the cabinet dimensions.

Decide what the island must do

A sink, cooktop, storage drawers and casual seating compete for space. Prioritize functions and coordinate plumbing, power and ventilation before construction.

Planning prompt: Write down three non-negotiable outcomes for the project, then ask which construction decisions are necessary to achieve each one.
