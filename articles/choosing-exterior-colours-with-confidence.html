Choosing Exterior Colours with Confidence

Evaluate colour alongside roof materials, masonry, trim, and daylight.

Retrospective topic: September 2025 · Oakwaters Journal

Exterior finishes appear different in full sun, shade, and overcast weather.

Test colours in real daylight

View samples on more than one elevation and at different times of day. Roof colour, brick, stone and neighbouring finishes can change how a paint colour reads.

Consider maintenance and contrast

Choose a coherent palette for siding, trim, doors and accents. Review finish durability, preparation requirements and any neighbourhood or heritage restrictions before committing.

Planning prompt: Write down three non-negotiable outcomes for the project, then ask which construction decisions are necessary to achieve each one.
