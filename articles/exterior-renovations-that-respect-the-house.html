Exterior Renovations That Respect the House

Coordinate siding, trim, entry details, and drainage as one system.

Retrospective topic: May 2024 · Oakwaters Journal

Replacing one exterior finish can reveal inconsistencies in proportion or details elsewhere on the facade.

Start with the home’s proportions

Look at window alignment, rooflines, entry details and material transitions. A successful update should feel coherent with the existing building rather than applied as a separate façade.

Protect the building envelope

Confirm flashing, air sealing, drainage and compatibility of exterior assemblies. Cosmetic improvements should not trap moisture or hide deteriorated materials.

Planning prompt: Write down three non-negotiable outcomes for the project, then ask which construction decisions are necessary to achieve each one.
