A Better Approach to Open-Concept Living

Use structure, lighting, and furniture zones to create connected spaces.

Retrospective topic: June 2025 · Oakwaters Journal

Removing walls can improve connection, but it can also reduce storage and acoustic separation.

Plan sightlines before removing walls

Map the views from the entry, kitchen, dining table and seating area. A wide opening may improve daylight but eliminate useful storage and places for furniture. Structural changes require professional assessment before any wall is removed.

Control sound, lighting and circulation

Open rooms need separate lighting zones and enough clearance around tables and seating. Plan where conversations, television sound and cooking noise will overlap.

Planning prompt: Write down three non-negotiable outcomes for the project, then ask which construction decisions are necessary to achieve each one.
