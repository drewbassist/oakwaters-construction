Bathroom Renovations Without Regrets

Make waterproofing, ventilation, and comfortable clearances part of the design conversation.

Retrospective topic: December 2023 · Oakwaters Journal

Tile and fixtures receive most of the attention, but concealed assemblies determine how well a bathroom performs over time.

Solve moisture and ventilation first

Before choosing tile, check existing signs of leaks, ventilation performance and waterproofing details. Discuss shower slopes, drain placement and the transitions between wet and dry surfaces.

Check storage and clearances

Mock up the space required for cabinet doors, toilet access and shower entry. Include accessible storage for everyday products instead of relying on a large vanity alone.

Planning prompt: Write down three non-negotiable outcomes for the project, then ask which construction decisions are necessary to achieve each one.
