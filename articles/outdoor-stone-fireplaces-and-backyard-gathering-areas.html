Outdoor Stone Fireplaces and Backyard Gathering Areas

Designing around the whole patio.

Oakwaters Construction · Southern Ontario guide

A masonry fireplace can anchor an outdoor room, but the surrounding patio, seating distances and sightlines should be planned at the same time. A beautiful hearth is less useful if it blocks circulation.

Plan the construction details

Review local restrictions and applicable approvals for outdoor fires. Design foundations, firebox and chimney components for the chosen system, with appropriate clearances from roofs, trees and other combustibles.

Think about long-term use

Integrate seating, storage, lighting and drainage early. Choose masonry materials and detailing that withstand weather exposure, and use qualified professionals for combustion and venting components.

Before starting work

Discuss the intended use, site conditions, materials, construction sequence and maintenance expectations with the project team. Verify permits and applicable requirements with the local municipality and qualified trades before ordering materials or beginning work. The best result comes from resolving practical details while the design is still flexible.
