Designing an Outdoor Living Area

Create outdoor zones that suit sun, wind, privacy, and seasonal use.

Retrospective topic: April 2025 · Oakwaters Journal

Outdoor rooms work best when their location and function are planned as carefully as interior spaces.

Organize outdoor activities into zones

Plan seating, dining, cooking and movement paths based on sun, wind and privacy. Locate furniture on a scaled plan to avoid undersized patios.

Choose materials for Ontario seasons

Discuss drainage, freeze-thaw exposure, slip resistance and storage for seasonal furniture. Exterior lighting and power are easiest to plan before hardscaping.

Planning prompt: Write down three non-negotiable outcomes for the project, then ask which construction decisions are necessary to achieve each one.
