The Renovation Walkthrough Checklist

Prepare for handover with an organized review of the finished work.

Retrospective topic: June 2026 · Oakwaters Journal

The final walkthrough is a chance to understand the renovated space and document remaining items.

Document conditions room by room

Photograph existing finishes, note cracks or moisture staining, measure key dimensions and identify awkward circulation. Record which elements should be retained.

Bring specific questions to the walkthrough

Ask about structural constraints, mechanical systems, access, permits, sequencing and material lead times. Keep a written list of assumptions that need confirmation.

Planning prompt: Write down three non-negotiable outcomes for the project, then ask which construction decisions are necessary to achieve each one.
