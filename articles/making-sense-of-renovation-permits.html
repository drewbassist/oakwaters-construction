Making Sense of Renovation Permits

Know when professional review and municipal approval may be required.

Retrospective topic: August 2025 · Oakwaters Journal

Permit requirements vary with the scope of work and local jurisdiction.

Identify work that may need approval

Structural changes, additions, certain plumbing alterations and changes in occupancy may trigger permits or inspections. Requirements depend on the municipality and the exact scope of work.

Confirm responsibility before work begins

Contact the local building department or consult a qualified designer about drawings, applications and inspection stages. Ask who will obtain permits and how approvals affect the construction schedule.

Planning prompt: Write down three non-negotiable outcomes for the project, then ask which construction decisions are necessary to achieve each one.
