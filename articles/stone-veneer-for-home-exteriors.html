Stone Veneer for Home Exteriors

Adding texture with correct detailing.

Oakwaters Construction · Southern Ontario guide

Stone veneer can give an entry, addition or exterior wall depth and character. Its success depends on proportion and detailing as much as on the selected colour and stone pattern.

Plan the construction details

Evaluate the wall assembly, structural support, water-resistive barrier, flashing, drainage and manufacturer requirements before choosing a veneer system. Details at windows, doors and grade are especially important.

Think about long-term use

Mock up a small section in daylight. Compare corner treatments, joint widths and transitions to siding or brick. Properly resolved edges make the installation feel intentional rather than applied.

Before starting work

Discuss the intended use, site conditions, materials, construction sequence and maintenance expectations with the project team. Verify permits and applicable requirements with the local municipality and qualified trades before ordering materials or beginning work. The best result comes from resolving practical details while the design is still flexible.
