Renovation Planning for Multigenerational Living

Balance independence, accessibility, privacy, and shared space.

Retrospective topic: October 2025 · Oakwaters Journal

Households with different generations often need both connection and separation.

Balance shared and private spaces

Map different household schedules, quiet zones, storage needs and bathroom access. Separate entrances or flexible living areas may improve independence where feasible.

Consider accessibility and local rules

Review door widths, thresholds, stair use and bathroom layouts. A secondary suite or change in occupancy may involve zoning, fire separation and building-code requirements.

Planning prompt: Write down three non-negotiable outcomes for the project, then ask which construction decisions are necessary to achieve each one.
