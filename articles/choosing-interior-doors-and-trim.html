Choosing Interior Doors and Trim

Use small architectural details to create a consistent interior language.

Retrospective topic: May 2026 · Oakwaters Journal

Door profiles, baseboards, and casing affect how finished a renovation feels.

Choose doors for function as well as style

Consider swing clearance, privacy, sound transmission and hardware. A pocket door can save floor space but requires an appropriate wall cavity and coordinated electrical layout.

Keep trim proportions consistent

Compare casing, baseboard and crown profiles against ceiling height and existing architectural details. Decide transitions before ordering to avoid awkward junctions.

Planning prompt: Write down three non-negotiable outcomes for the project, then ask which construction decisions are necessary to achieve each one.
